## Working subprocessor register

current product architecture uses or may use Xero, Stripe, Sinch, VoIPcloud,
AWS Cognito, OVHcloud, VentraIP/email delivery, Sophiie and OpenAI. The final
register must state each provider's purpose and the likely processing country
or region without exposing credentials or internal account identifiers.
