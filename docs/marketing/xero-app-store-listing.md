## App name

AccountPulse

## Tagline

Automated invoice reminders that help Australian businesses get paid sooner.

## Short description

Connect AccountPulse to Xero to organise overdue invoices, review or automate
payment reminders, and keep SMS conversations and reminder activity together.

## Full description

AccountPulse helps Australian businesses follow up unpaid Xero invoices with a
clear, auditable workflow.

Connect one Xero organisation, sync customer and invoice information, then use
staged reminder sequences across SMS and Xero email. Start in review-and-approve
mode and enable automation only for the sequences you choose. AccountPulse also
provides an inbox and outbox, manual SMS and email actions, call shortcuts,
escalation tasks, exclusions and contact-detail exception reporting.

Solo includes one dedicated Australian SMS reply number and up to 1,000 outbound
SMS parts in each monthly allowance period. One message can use more than one
SMS part depending on its length and characters. Unused parts expire at the end
of the allowance period. Additional outbound SMS parts are billed at A$0.11 each
excluding GST. Unused included parts and purchased overage do not roll over.

## Key features

- Standard Xero OAuth connection and guided initial sync
- Review-and-approve or sequence-level automatic reminders
- SMS reminders with a dedicated Australian reply number
- Xero email reminders and manual email actions
- Customer reply inbox and auditable message outbox
- Manual SMS, call and escalation actions
- Client and invoice exclusions
- Search, sorting and contact-detail exception reporting
- Australian business-hours and daily contact-frequency controls

## Pricing

AccountPulse Solo costs A$299 per month excluding GST. Annual billing is
available with a 5% discount. Payment is due during signup and there is no public
free trial. The plan includes one Xero organisation, one dedicated Australian
SMS reply number and up to 1,000 outbound SMS parts per monthly allowance
period. Additional outbound SMS parts cost A$0.11 each excluding GST.
Telephone-number fees are not refundable.

## Availability and support

AccountPulse is currently offered to Australian businesses in Australian
dollars. Support and account enquiries: ap@motts.com.au.

## Onboarding summary

1. Create an AccountPulse account and accept the current legal terms.
2. Choose monthly or annual billing and complete the first card payment.
3. Connect the authorised Xero organisation through Xero OAuth.
4. Complete the initial Xero sync and onboarding checklist.
5. Wait for the dedicated SMS number to be activated before live SMS sending.
6. Review reminder settings and choose which sequences may run automatically.

Number activation can require provider compliance review. AccountPulse must show
the provisioning status and must not promise immediate SMS activation before the
number is ready.
